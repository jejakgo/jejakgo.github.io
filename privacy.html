JejakGo – Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

JejakGo is a journey planner. You plan a journey step by step (or let Google suggest one), and the app guides you with GPS and time alarms so you don't miss your stop. This page explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your journeys, places and photos are stored only on your phone.
  • Your location is used while you plan or travel, to work out routes and to wake you before your stop.
  • To calculate routes, show maps and find places, the app sends the places involved to Google.
  • JejakGo has no servers of its own. We never receive, sell or share your data.

Location

JejakGo uses your precise location (GPS) to:

  • use "My location" as a starting point when you plan;
  • follow your progress during a journey you start, and ring the "get ready" and stop alarms;
  • warn you if you are far from the planned route.

During a journey the app keeps tracking with a visible notification, also when the screen is off. Tracking stops when the journey ends or when you stop it. JejakGo does not ask for location "all the time" and does not track you when no journey is running. Your location history is not stored or sent anywhere except as described below.

Google services

JejakGo uses Google services to plan and show journeys:

  • Google Routes API – the start and end points of a piece of your journey (and the time) are sent to Google to get walking, driving, cycling, motorbike and public-transport routes and departure times.
  • Google Maps SDK – shows maps in the app.
  • Android place search (Geocoder) – turns the place names you type into locations.
  • Google Maps links – if you share or paste a Google Maps link, the app opens it to read the location.

Google handles these requests under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Journeys, places and photos

  • Journeys you save, your named places ("Home", "Mum's house") and your recent trips are stored on your phone only.
  • Photos of landmarks you add to a step are stored in the app on your phone. The app only uses the camera or your photos when you tap to add one.
  • When you choose Share, the app creates a .jejakgo file with that journey (steps, notes, photos and place names) and hands it to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp or email). You decide who receives it.

Notifications and alarms

JejakGo shows a notification during a journey and rings alarms (also on the lock screen) to wake you before your stop and to remind you when to leave. These are created on your phone and are not sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

You can delete journeys and places in the app at any time. Uninstalling JejakGo removes everything it stored on your phone.

Children

JejakGo is a general travel app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
